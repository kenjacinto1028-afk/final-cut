# Final Cut v13 — Release Changelog

Released November 20, 2026. Builds on all released v12 systems; game save version **13**.

## A — More selective industry honors
- Applies the same nomination and award-merit thresholds to player films and AI films, without a hidden AI entry exemption.
- Raises standards for Golden Reel, Critics Circle and Audience Spotlight winners. Indie awards use a lower but still meaningful threshold; strong shorts and lower-budget films can still qualify for elite festivals on merit.
- Elite festivals require credible competition for their grand prize, while Local and Regional circuits remain accessible; preserves jury mentions, selections and distinct festival awards.
- Awards campaigns add a smaller competitive edge, never a guaranteed trophy. All existing ceremonies and festivals remain; individual prizes remain restricted to actors, directors and writers.

## B — Fame, momentum and lasting legacy
- **Lifetime Fame:** durable recognition for career stature, separate from present-day headlines.
- **Current Buzz:** rises with releases/hits/awards, falls after flops and prolonged quiet periods; positive gains now diminish as hype rises.
- **Current Prestige:** rolling five-year critical/award standing with weighted historical decay; less likely to saturate at 100.
- **Lifetime Legacy:** accumulates verified career achievements and never decays. Recent prestige and lifetime legacy both contribute to the final score.

## C — Stronger living rival world
- **6 starting rivals, 15 active maximum**; 3–4 studios emerge as industry leaders based on actual trailing film results, awards and quality.
- Named rival studio identities and preferred genres influence production; leading rivals benefit most in their genuine specialty and from the skill of their credited talent, not from arbitrary blanket bonuses.
- Rolling 3-/5-year studio standings, yearbook records, decade champions and rise/fall headlines.

## D — Optional late-game ambitions
- Four occasional, paid initiatives: prestige passion production, franchise expansion, global collaboration, and talent development. They have cooldowns, risks and outcomes without weekly management spam.
- Historic hit-film reissues provide limited fresh revenue and audience attention, with eligibility and repeat limits; the original theatrical gross stays unchanged.
- Decade shifts affect genre appeal and existing market mechanics, creating opportunities for challengers.
- Permanent studio milestones record meaningful achievements; the endgame recap highlights defining films, favorite directors/writers and leading rivals.

## E — Readable interface
- Updated dashboard shows fame, buzz, current prestige and lifetime legacy, optional initiatives, revival options and current industry shifts.
- Rivals page shows industry leaders, rankings and decade champions; awards pages explain competitive rules and years with no qualifying winner.
- Score view separates live reputation from permanent achievement and adds a **Final Studio Legacy Recap** at score lock.
- Mobile layout and compressed save migration preserved; v12 saves carry forward without inventing absent historical credits.

## F — Testing and balancing
- Tuning limits prestige saturation, softens buzz gains, makes elite awards selectively harder, and makes AI team skill count when forming rival films.
- **20 full multi-region campaigns**, **19 reached 2031**, **one legitimate bankruptcy**; all 20 compressed saves reloaded under a simulated 5 MiB browser quota.
- Re-ran 53 preexisting gameplay regression checks plus v13 engine tests, three era snapshots and browser/mobile UI checks. Detailed results in `TEST_REPORT_V13.md` and `V13_BALANCE_F1.md`.

## Known limitations
Prolific late-game studios can still sustain high Current Buzz, although Current Prestige now varies materially. Elite trophies are less automatic but award collections remain large over a 61-year history. Real GitHub Pages caching and cross-device poster persistence need hands-on verification after publication.
