# FinalCut v14.3 — Film Lifecycle, Promotion & Guided First Film
**Status:** Proposed next update — planning only; no gameplay code implemented or approved for release.
**Baseline:** v14.2 on \`kenjacinto1028-afk/final-cut/main\` (commit \`37d827a\`), retaining save schema v13 and v14.1 balance unless measured tuning supports change.
**Design:** Meaningful film- and studio-management decisions with minimal micromanagement, a living industry, and controls discoverable when unavailable as well as available.

## Source audit: what exists, what is missing

The user's screenshots show (1) charts with almost no numeric labels, (2) a custom poster **already visible at the top**, followed by an unrelated procedurally generated *film still*, and (3) World Film Archive's \`$0\` theatrical box-office column for a short. Audit conclusions:

- \`ui.js\` \`filmPulseChart\` has \`title\` hover text, but no always-visible values or mobile-friendly keyboard/touch detail.
- Film-detail drawer renders \`posterFor(f)\` and then **always** \`Media.filmStillSVG(f)\`, even when a custom poster is applied. Uploaded image persistence is a separate browser-storage concern; don't infer that the upload itself failed from the screenshot.
- \`filmTable(rows, ranked, mine)\` already has detailed studio receipts, direct P/L, reach estimates and non-theatrical \`—\` formatting **only when viewing My Filmography**. World Film Archive shows only box office for all formats.
- Film decisions, film-performance panels, v14 release response and afterlife are **in the film/project drawer**. Some controls appear only during eligible states, so tutorial text currently points to a page where the named decision may not be visible. Short-film press kit/encore is a different path than completed-feature afterlife.
- \`shortReleaseAction\` uses native \`confirm()\`; the current quoted press kit adds **+4 future festival merit**, never a guaranteed invitation, selection or win.
- \`Engine.v14ReleaseCalendar()\` includes **only player feature films**, rivals are currently feature-only, and the UI doesn't expose film format/genre/topic match warnings; both actual player releases and rival films should be distinguishable without inventing AI shorts.
- Existing \`TUNING.marketing\`, post-opening publicity, release positioning and early response already influence hype and costs. v14.3 must extend/clarify these without stacking duplicate free bonuses.
- v14 feature afterlife requires **a completed feature**, at least **4 weeks since release**, may be blocked by critic/audience/rights requirements, and is limited to two opportunities with a one-year cooldown. Short-film encore generally requires **quality 60+ or festival selection** and a finished initial circuit. A 44-quality short without a selection should see why it is ineligible; do not promise options it cannot access.

## Shared gameplay decisions and constraints

**Promotion:** Early film awareness should be a tradeoff: a low-cost local/basic campaign may be viable for a short; targeted and broad campaigns cost proportionally more and interact with topic, lead fame, budgets, existing marketing and release strategy. Limit repeated actions and total extra hype; distinguish **pre-release marketing**, **release strategy**, **post-opening push**, and **festival press kit**. Sponsorship is an independent funding/brand partnership, not another hidden marketing multiplier. Offer transparency and allow declining without penalty.

**Sponsors:** Unlock *availability and tier*, not success, from established Lifetime Fame (long-term), Current Buzz (current visibility) and Current Prestige (credibility). Each genuine offer has a fictional sponsor name, eligible media formats, genre/topic fit, upfront amount, placement/publicity requirement, exposure benefit, deadline and visible artistic/reputation downside. Low-fame studios should not rely on sponsor money to survive. Sponsorship must not allow infinite money printing or override credits/profit accounting.

**Critic reviews:** Use small, original, era-appropriate review fragments chosen from bounded authored templates and film attributes; persist generated names, tiers, ratings and text so save/reload and historical archives are stable. Initial 1–2 lower-tier reviews; further reviews can arrive later depending on interest and film performance. Cap both **positive and negative** hype changes; an independent aggregate critics score remains grounded in existing film evaluation, while publication ratings can vary around it. Avoid buying acclaim with marketing/sponsorship. Review access should not make festival awards automatic.

**Discoverability:** A disabled or hidden opportunity must not be mistaken for a missing system. Each relevant feature should show **why and when**: e.g., “Theatrical performance available after feature premiere”, “No festival judging remains”, “This short does not qualify for an encore yet; needs quality 60+ or a festival selection”, or “Feature catalog actions begin four weeks after release”. Show the current stage and actionable next step. Keep the interface uncluttered with expandable “Future opportunities” rather than a huge list of grayed-out buttons.

**Data integrity:** Preserve v13 saves, historical project finances, archived rivals, custom posters and no-cloud/local-browser storage. New critic/sponsor data should be optional on older saves, never retroactively fabricate old reviews, revenue, attendance or commercial deals. Film gross ≠ studio receipts; direct P/L excludes shared overhead, as already explained in the game.

## Acceptance criteria / tests

1. **Full realistic starter path:** fresh 1970 studio with a cheap short can select a script, choose a reasonable marketing level, greenlight, click Next week/Advance to next decision, handle a real dilemma only if it happens, complete screenings, review earnings/P&L, discover archives and know why encore/press kit may be unavailable. All tutorial targets exist at the moment used.
2. **Richer career path:** successful feature gets visible planned release and film promotion; when actually eligible, theatrical performance and optional response appear in the same film context; after completion, the catalog panel explains the four-week and quality/rights gates.
3. **Sponsorship balance:** poor new studio typically has zero available offers; established studios progressively attract more/tiered offers; sponsor costs, benefits, fit and risks are recorded exactly once; refusing deals remains viable; high-end studios cannot exploit repeated cash windfalls.
4. **Review integrity:** seeded small studio produces 1–2 lower-tier reviews initially; larger studio receives varied higher-tier attention, not guaranteed acclaim; ratings/text are stable after save/load; positive/negative hype impact bounded; no retroactive reviews for legacy releases.
5. **Release competition:** actual player shorts/features/pilots appear with correct format and dates; rival features appear with correct titles and truthful warnings for actual shared genres/topics. No phantom planned rival short release.
6. **Charts, archive and posters:** 320/390px mobile and desktop show readable numbers, focus/tap access, no horizontal overflow; short theatrical gross displays \`—\`, while screening receipts/reach remain visible. Custom poster is consistently rendered; optional generated still cannot masquerade as a second poster.
7. **Festival confirmation:** confirm/cancel/repeat tests guarantee the kit costs once and raises only **future** jury merit; no unearned award or selection.
8. **Regression and release:** no balance exploits, no duplicated cash/awards, same v13 save schema, archive and poster persistence; multi-seed short/feature/pilot campaigns and long careers checked. GitHub PR/main CI, Pages deployment and linked changelog passed. Physical Android/iOS remains a distinct manual validation item.
