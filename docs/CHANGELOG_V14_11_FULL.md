# FinalCut v14.11a–f — A Living Production Lifecycle
Date: 2026-11-22

## v14.11a — Film Management Hub
- One shared horizontally scrollable action row in the Production Center, unreleased Project Details and released Film Hub.
- **Promotions, Budget, Distribution, Cast & Crew, Release, Festivals, Decisions, Legacy** are distinct panes; the current production stage indicates which actions can be used.
- Buttons reuse actual engine quotes and confirmations for production budget, campaign/sponsor offers, region selection, release strategy, short-film festivals and existing catalog decisions. Unavailable stages display their requirements rather than creating free actions.
- The Decisions pane shows saved choice, week, production phase and actual consequence. Cast & Crew shows studio relationship ratings.
- The compact stage-first Project Monitor and progressive detail tabs from the previous readability update remain in place.

## v14.11b — Development and pre-production
- Four distinct development event templates: creative ending, research, sales pitch and story rights.
- Four pre-production templates: location scheduling, expanded role, sponsor placement and technical risk.
- Each includes consequential choices; genre/topic-sensitive examples and recurring characters use existing film context.

## v14.11c — Filming and post-production
- Filming includes performer chemistry, equipment setbacks, weather, artistic disputes, and optional follow-ups when a preceding role or safety decision created a dependency.
- Post-production adds editing, music, test screenings and late rights clearance, with story changes, expenditure and scheduling outcomes.
- The engine records story tags across stages; an earlier technical risk or rights decision can produce a later payoff. Projects pause stage advancement when the player receives an unresolved lifecycle choice.

## v14.11d — Before release, release and after release
- Three prerelease templates: trailer traction, rival opening conflict and cast publicity.
- Three live release templates: word of mouth, critic controversy and earned niche-fan campaigns.
- Four after-release opportunities: new rights interest, sequel/adaptation brainstorming, reunions and cult retrospectives.
- Earned sequel/reunion/cult tags can modestly change a future related film's initial hype or catalog eligibility. The original film's success does **not** guarantee a sequel's success, sponsorship, festival selection or licensing proceeds.
- After-premiere choices update recorded film hype and story history. Existing rights and catalog actions remain paid and conditional.

## v14.11e — Persistent talent relationships
- Creative choices can strengthen or strain relationships between the studio and involved cast and crew.
- Talent bond defaults to neutral **50/100** for existing talent. Events change bond in small increments; normal relationships can modestly affect later performance (at most ±3 skill points) and future negotiated fees (at most ±5%).
- Roster morale is also influenced by consequential choices, while existing long-term director/writer partnership mechanics remain in place.

## v14.11f — Event frequency, delegation and reliability
- One event per early phase in most cases, with an optional second filming event for features; soft two-week cooldown, per-film limit **five for shorts/pilots and six for features**, with an opportunity reserved for the film's post-release life.
- The existing creative delegation policy works with this system: routine choices can be delegated to director/commercial strategy; major sponsorship, artistic conflict, rights, competition and sequel direction decisions still require the player.
- Savings from production choices reduce **future** recorded film spending; they never create free cash. Spending still uses allocated budgets and real studio funds.
- Existing saved films and talent load without new fields: neutral bond and empty lifecycle history are created only as needed. Save version remains 13.
- Tests: deterministic cross-stage choice chains, normal decision queue, genre/topic critic voices, retained save/load, desktop and phone action controls, and two **funded 12-year simulation careers** with event caps and financial-integrity checks. Funded stress outcomes should not be confused with economic balance results for a new studio.

## Where to find it
**Production Center → select a film → Manage**, or **Project Details / Film Hub → Manage**. Use **Decisions** for resolved lifecycle opportunities and **Cast & Crew** for current studio relationships. Time-sensitive event popups appear in the existing decision queue and Next Action.
