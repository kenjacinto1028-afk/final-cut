# Final Cut v13 — Gameplay Impact

| Improvement | What the player notices | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Buzz separate from fame | Even a famous studio has to keep releasing successful films to remain in the spotlight | Long campaigns retain momentum goals |
| Rolling prestige | Recent critically acclaimed films and major wins matter more than a 50-year-old trophy | Recognition can rise or fade without erasing history |
| Permanent legacy | Milestones and proven achievements never disappear | Every era contributes to the enduring studio story |
| More demanding top awards | Some major categories have no qualifying winner; indie awards stay more attainable | Elite wins mean more; small studios still have paths to recognition |
| Rival leaders | Three or four recognizable competitors rise and fall within 15 active rivals | Greater world variety without save bloat |
| Studio leaderboards | Three-/five-year standings plus decade champions | Concrete late-game competitive goals |
| Studio initiatives | Occasional costly passion, franchise, global and talent-development choices | Meaningful risks without repetitive weekly popups |
| Classic-film reissues | One bounded nostalgia opportunity for eligible historic releases | Old films remain relevant without infinite income |
| Industry shifts | Decade-specific temporary changes in genre audiences | Established formulas periodically need adapting |
| Final legacy recap | Defining films, directors/writers and rivals shown at score lock | A stronger conclusion than cash and a single number |

### Balance conclusions
In 20 deterministic seeded campaigns over five regions and four strategies, 19 reached the 2031 scoring milestone, while one blockbuster run failed financially in 2019. Current Prestige finished 24–65 (mean 52.1), versus prior late-game saturation near 100. Current Buzz remained 82–98 for actively releasing studios; it decays substantially during inactivity. The rival world stayed at 8–15 active studios and archived about 2,176–2,788 rival films per run. The existing player experience and saved historical records are preserved.
