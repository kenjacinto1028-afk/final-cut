# Final Cut v12 — Complete Gameplay Impact

| Feature | What the player does differently | Strategic effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent skill and fame | Compare creative craft and market popularity separately when casting | Unknown experts can make better films; celebrities can sell weaker films |
| Recognition tiers | See Local through Global labels for actors, directors and writers; cast freely | Tier informs publicity and negotiations, never blocks hiring |
| Career progression | Revisit talent to monitor rising skill, slumps and comebacks | Better long-term talent scouting and contract timing |
| Quality breakdown | Inspect skill, genre/topic, budget, staffing and decision contributions | Film outcomes are easier to understand and improve |
| Rival identities | Compete against studios specializing in particular genres and changing tactics | More strategic release timing and genre specialization |
| Better film reviews | Read contextual reasons for hits, flops and cult favorites | Film history gains explanation, not just scores |
| TV/economy refinements | Evaluate renewals using ratings momentum, profitability and forecast fees | More informed choices; salary and wage pressure remain real |
| Sequel continuity | Re-sign original stars/director/writer when practical, or deliberately recast | Retention protects built-in hype; recasting may improve craft but costs publicity |
| Optional supporting cast | Use additional performer slots on bigger productions | More expressive casts and possible publicity with fees and diminishing returns |
| Optional specialist crew | Hire extra named technical staff/background package if worth the fee | Production reliability and craftsmanship may rise; no forced micromanagement |
| Director/writer biographies | Open full filmographies, honors and grounded career events | Creative collaborators become recognizable personalities with lasting histories |
| Personal award attribution | See acting, direction and writing honors credited to actual artists | Awards affect the correct careers; Best Picture stays a film trophy |
| Large-save compaction | Normal autosaving is more economical as the world film archive grows | Reduces risk of losing progress to browser storage quotas in decades-long games |

**Balance effects and limits:** Quality remains independent of fame. Large crews cannot guarantee a masterpiece; cast and contract fees cut into the production budget. Fame and prestige gains are more restrained at high values, but both can still reach 100 in long, successful campaigns. Low-budget and prestige strategies remain playable. Bankruptcy remains possible and is not treated as a test crash.

**Usability:** No mandatory individual employee management added. Routine department staff are aggregate headcount/skill/morale; actors and actresses retain compact profiles. Only directors and writers get full career timelines. Poster art, mobile appearance, and backup management from v11 remain available.

**Testing disclosure:** 53 current/compatibility engine checks (10 v12 smoke + 12 Batch F + 10 v9 + 12 v10 + 9 v11); 8 multi-region full campaigns; browser checks. The GitHub Pages live-origin IndexedDB persistence scenario remains outside the local browser sandbox and should be tested on the deployed site.
